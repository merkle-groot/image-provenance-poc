Preparing images

Each watermarked PNG and MP4 file downloads separately. Your browser may ask you to allow multiple downloads.

Watermarking is experimental. Files must be under the upload limit (50 MB by default); videos need even frame dimensions and at least 80 × 80 pixels; video audio is re-encoded. This demo runs on a low-end server, so video processing is slow; the finished PoC will be a lot faster.
